# Privacy policy — Yes, Delete It

_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

**Yes, Delete It doesn't collect, store or transmit any personal or browsing data.**

- It reads the current page only when you click its toolbar button or press its
  keyboard shortcut, and only to find "type … to confirm" fields on that page.
- The text it detects stays in that tab's memory while the key is on screen. It is never
  saved and never sent anywhere.
- It makes no network requests. No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, ads or accounts.
- It stores two preferences in your browser's extension storage ("show the key" or
  "type directly", and whether to show the Copy button). If you're signed in to your
  browser, the browser itself may sync them between your devices; the developer never
  receives them.
- No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone, for any purpose.

Permissions and why they're needed:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `activeTab` | Read the current tab only after you click the button or press the shortcut |
| `scripting` | Insert the detector into that tab |
| `storage` | Save the two preferences above |

## Política de privacidad (español)

**Yes, Delete It no recoge, guarda ni transmite ningún dato personal ni de navegación.**

Solo lee la página actual cuando pulsas su botón o su atajo, y únicamente para encontrar
campos de "escribe … para confirmar". El texto detectado se queda en la memoria de esa
pestaña mientras la tecla está en pantalla; nunca se guarda ni se envía. No hace
peticiones de red. Guarda dos preferencias en el almacenamiento de extensiones de tu
navegador. No se vende ni se comparte ningún dato.
